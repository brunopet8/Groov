Tiny 16px mono label for counts, versions and states inside panels and headers.

```jsx
<Badge>27.15.0</Badge>
<Badge tone="red" dot>Live</Badge>
<Badge tone="rose" variant="outline">Source</Badge>
<Badge tone="red" variant="solid">3</Badge>
```

- `tone` — neutral · red · rose · white.
- `variant` — soft (tint) · solid · outline.
